📖 About MarketDx

Learn about the platform and its capabilities

What is MarketDx?
MarketDx is a market diagnosis platform providing structural intelligence for Indian equity markets (NSE & BSE). We focus on analysis, not prediction. Our AI-powered engine diagnoses market conditions using 8 regimes, 10 patterns, and multiple technical indicators.
Key Features
✓ Real-time market diagnosis
✓ Stock pattern recognition
✓ NSE vs BSE comparison
✓ Explainable AI insights
✓ Offline support (PWA)
✓ Educational content
✓ Mobile optimized
Academic Framework
MarketDx was developed as a college project with guidance from Dr. B. SUJATHA. The platform emphasizes structural analysis and educational value over speculation. All diagnosis is based on historical data and technical patterns, with no predictive claims.
